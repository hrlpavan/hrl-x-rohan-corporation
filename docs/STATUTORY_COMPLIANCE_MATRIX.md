### FEMA Key Directives:
1. **Eligible Purchase:** NRIs/OCIs are permitted without prior RBI permission to acquire immovable commercial and residential properties in India under Master Direction No. 12/2015-16.
2. **Repatriation Entitlement:** Sale proceeds of commercial and residential property acquired through foreign exchange inward remittances (NRE) are repatriable outside India up to two residential properties, subject to taxes.
3. **Automated Documentation:** The platform automatically pre-fills Form A2 and RBI inward remittance checklist templates upon transaction initiation.
