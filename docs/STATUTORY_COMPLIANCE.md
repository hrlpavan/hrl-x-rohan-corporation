# Statutory Governance, RERA & Legal Standards

## 1. Statutory Grounding

This joint technological initiative adheres to the governing legal standards of the Republic of India and the State of Karnataka:

1. **Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA)**:
   - Complete statutory transparency regarding approved sanctions, carpet area disclosures, and construction milestones for all featured Rohan Corporation developments.
   - Every project referenced in the platform reflects verified RERA registration status.

2. **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP)**:
   - Strict adherence to data minimization principles for buyer inquiries, virtual tour bookings, and smart home usage logs.
   - Resident biometric data remains on physical on-premise hardware and is never synced to external third-party cloud servers.

3. **Trade Marks Act, 1999 & Indian Copyright Act, 1957**:
   - `HRL™` and associated computational architectures are proprietary to HRL International Private Limited.
   - `Rohan Corporation™` trademarks, building renders, and architectural plans remain the sole property of Rohan Corporation.
