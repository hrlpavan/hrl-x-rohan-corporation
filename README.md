For over three decades, **Rohan Corporation** has shaped Coastal Karnataka's skyline through iconic commercial, residential, and plotted communities. **HRL International Private Limited**, founded by **Pavan Kumar Sadashiv**, introduces advanced computational intelligence to these physical assets:

1. **Photorealistic Architectural Digital Twins**: WebGL / Canvas interactive daylight, acoustic, and spatial simulations.

3. **Global NRI Investment & Yield Telemetry**: Real-time property valuation, RERA statutory tracking, and automated ROI simulators for prospective buyers and the Mangalorean diaspora worldwide.
