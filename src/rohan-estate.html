Civil Engineering Excellence

A World-Class Master Layout. Built for Generations.

Every inch of Rohan Estate is constructed with meticulous engineering standards to ensure lifelong endurance, effortless maintenance, and sustainable tranquility.

UNDERGROUND UTILITIES

Zero Overhead Wires

All high-voltage electrical distribution, street lighting cables, fiber telecommunications, and pressurized water mains run in reinforced underground service conduits. The result is unhindered skyline vistas and complete protection against monsoon storms.

CIVIL BOULEVARDS

40ft & 30ft Tarred Avenues

Heavy-duty asphalted roads engineered with structured sub-base layers, kerb stones, concrete interlocking pedestrian footpaths, and scientifically sloped storm-water box drains that eliminate monsoon water stagnation entirely.

Perimeter Enclosure

Gated entrance portal with 24/7 security guards, boom barriers, and round-the-clock CCTV surveillance across all avenue intersections.
